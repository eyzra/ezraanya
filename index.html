Who is Ezra Anya?

A clear voice for people and the brands they build.

Ezra leads brand strategy and messaging for a pan-African consulting firm and a digital telecommunications company. Founders, institutions and personal brands come to him for counsel on corporate communications, media and PR, and on bringing AI into their work responsibly.

Outside the boardroom he teaches. He writes on theology, psychology and personal growth, hosts a monthly gathering for serious thinkers and has walked more than a hundred young people through his mentorship programme.
