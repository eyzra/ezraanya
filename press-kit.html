Full bio

Ezra Anya is a brand, communications and AI strategist who helps people see, think and believe. He leads brand strategy and messaging for organisations across several industries, most of all a pan-African consulting firm and a digital telecommunications company. Founders, institutions and personal brands turn to him for counsel on corporate communications, media and PR, and on adopting AI with clear ethical guardrails.

He graduated with first-class honours in Cybersecurity from the Federal University of Technology, Minna, where he won the Vice Chancellor's Award for Academic and Leadership Excellence four times. He also trained in AI and Media at the University of Oxford's Saïd Business School.

Ezra hosts Reflections W/ TEA, a monthly online teaching on faith and personal growth, and he founded TheThriveTribe, a mentorship platform that has guided more than a hundred young people.
