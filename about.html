About

Thinker.Storyteller.Builder.

Ezra speaks, tells stories and brings things to life. He works with products that need a strategy, people who need structure and ideas that deserve to be seen.

The story so far

Where strategy meets teaching.

Ezra is a brand, communications and AI strategist. He leads brand strategy and messaging for organisations across several industries, most of all a pan-African consulting firm and a digital telecommunications company.

He advises founders, institutions and personal brands on corporate communications, media and PR, and brand strategy. He builds the campaigns and video stories that carry those strategies to the public.

He holds a first-class degree in Cybersecurity, and it shows in how he thinks about systems, trust and risk. That training drives his work on AI ethics. He helps leaders bring AI into daily work with policies people can follow, workflows that save real hours and human judgment at every sign-off.

Beyond his client work he founded TheThriveTribe, a mentorship platform where young people learn self-leadership and personal development. He writes and speaks on psychology, philosophy and theology, and he keeps a close eye on global brand communications, narrative strategy and the growing role of AI in creative work.

What drives the work

One mandate,
in three moves.

01 · Make all men see

Illumination

The first work is always light. People get the clarity to perceive what was always there and never had a name.

02 · Make all men do

Impact

Clarity without action is decoration. Every teaching, story and strategy is designed to move people to act.

03 · Make all men become

Influence

The deepest work is formation. It is the slow, faithful shaping of who a person is becoming, one conversation at a time.

How Ezra works with AI

Fast tools,
human judgment.

AI speeds up research, pressure-tests messaging and widens the range of early ideas. Judgment, taste and accountability stay with Ezra, so clients get faster turnaround and more options before anything reaches them.

01

Brief

AI helps
Turns call notes into a first draft of the brief.
Ezra decides
The real problem and what success looks like.
02

Research

AI helps
Scans markets, competitors and coverage at speed.
Ezra decides
Which sources hold up and what matters.
03

Ideate

AI helps
Widens the field with more angles and concepts.
Ezra decides
The ideas that fit the brand and the moment.
04

Draft

AI helps
Produces first drafts of copy, scripts and structure.
Ezra decides
The rewrite for voice, nuance and local context.
05

Refine

AI helps
Tests messages against audiences and likely objections.
Ezra decides
The final calls on tone and risk.
06

Deliver

AI helps
Formats assets for each platform and channel.
Ezra decides
Sign-off on every piece, and he owns the result.
i.

iii.

Ezra reviews and signs every piece before it leaves his desk.

iv.

Fair and local

Outputs get checked for bias and for the context of the people they serve.
