What it is

Growth with a tribe around you.

TheThriveTribe is a mentorship platform for young people focused on self-leadership and personal development.

Most people know what they should change. They stall because nobody walks with them long enough for the change to hold. The tribe gives you three months of guidance, honest feedback and peers who expect you to show up.

More than a hundred young people have come through it so far.

The 90-day sprint

Three months,
one direction.

Each month builds on the last. You start by getting honest about where you are and finish with habits and a plan that outlast the programme.

01Month one · Days 1 to 30

See clearly

Take an honest look at where you are. Name your values, your patterns and the beliefs that have been running your choices.

02Month two · Days 31 to 60

Build daily

Turn insight into routine. Shape the habits, the discipline and the use of time that carry you when motivation runs out.

03Month three · Days 61 to 90

Lead forward

Set your direction for the next season. Leave with goals you can defend, a plan you can follow and people who will hold you to it.

Who it is for

You, if you are ready.

  • Students and young professionals who want structure for their growth
  • People who feel stuck between knowing and doing
  • Anyone who wants a mentor's honest view of their direction
  • Those willing to give the work ninety days of real attention
