What Synergy is

A partner
in the room.

Synergy is the deeper consultancy relationship.

Ezra works alongside the founder, the executive team or the organisation to address challenges that connect to each other and to drive the priorities that matter most. He learns how the organisation thinks, contributes to important decisions and helps move agreed priorities forward.

your priorities.

The investment

Starting from
₦2,000,000.

Synergy runs for a minimum of six months. The final investment is set in a written proposal and depends on the factors listed here.

  • The organisation's size and complexity
  • The number of strategic areas involved
  • The duration and intensity of the engagement
  • The number of leaders, teams or departments involved
  • The level of implementation support and accountability required
  • The expected deliverables and reporting requirements
